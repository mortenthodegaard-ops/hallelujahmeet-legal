Support

Help for the HallelujahMeet iOS and Android apps.

Restore an ad-free subscription

Open Profile in the app and choose Remove ads. Apple manages iOS billing in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, while Google manages Android billing in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Language and automatic translation

Open Profile → Language to choose the language for Bible verses, devotionals and shared prayers. Automatically translated prayers can be switched back to the original text from the prayer card.

Delete your account and data

On iOS, open Profile → Privacy & account → Delete my account. On Android, open Profile → Delete anonymous account. This removes the anonymous account, prayers, activity, settings and stored push values.

Report a prayer

Open the prayer, use the report action and confirm. Reported content enters moderation and is hidden after the reporting thresholds are reached.

Privacy choices

Open Profile → Privacy & account on iOS, or the privacy and notification rows in Profile on Android, to revisit advertising consent, notification settings, location controls and the privacy policy.

Contact

For help or a privacy request, email mortenthodegaard@hotmail.com. Please do not include sensitive prayer details unless necessary.
