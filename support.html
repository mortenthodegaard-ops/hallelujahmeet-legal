Support

Help for the HallelujahMeet iOS app.

Restore an ad-free subscription

Open Profile in the app, choose Remove ads, then select Restore purchases. Apple manages billing and cancellation in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Delete your account and data

Open Profile → Privacy & account → Delete my account. This removes the anonymous account, prayers, activity, settings and stored push values.

Report a prayer

Open the prayer, use the report action and confirm. Reported content enters moderation and is hidden after the reporting thresholds are reached.

Privacy choices

Open Profile → Privacy & account to revisit advertising consent, notification settings, location controls and the privacy policy.

Contact

For help or a privacy request, email mortenthodegaard@hotmail.com. Please do not include sensitive prayer details unless necessary.
