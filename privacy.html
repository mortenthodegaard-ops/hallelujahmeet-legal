Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026

Who operates the app

HallelujahMeet is operated by the developer identified on its Apple App Store listing. Privacy and support enquiries can be sent through the support page.

Anonymous account and profile

The app creates an anonymous Firebase account on first launch. It does not require your name, email address or phone number. You may choose a display name; it does not need to be your real name. The account identifier is used to keep your prayers, activity, settings and push tokens separate from those of other users.

Prayer content and activity

Prayer requests you publish are visible to other users. They include the text, category, language, publication time and, only if you choose it, an approximate location. We also store the prayers you have prayed for or reported so the app can prevent duplicate actions and show your history. Do not include personal information about yourself or others in public prayer text.

Location

Location sharing is optional and off by default. If you enable “Show on the prayer map,” the coordinate is rounded on your device to an area of approximately 25 kilometres before it is uploaded. The app never stores your precise coordinate.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you enable remote notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging stores an app-installation identifier and a push registration value so messages can reach your device. Turning remote notifications off removes the stored push value from your account.

Analytics and advertising

Google Analytics for Firebase measures app launches, sessions and feature interactions so we can understand whether the app works well. Google Mobile Ads may process device or app identifiers, IP-derived general location, ad impressions, interactions, diagnostics and performance information to deliver and measure advertising. In regions where consent is required, Google’s consent form is shown before ads are requested and provides privacy choices that can be reopened from the app. HallelujahMeet does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the IDFA.

We use Google as a service provider for Firebase, analytics and advertising. Data practices for these SDKs are also described in Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not sell personal information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app receives purchase status and transaction identifiers needed to provide the ad-free entitlement and restore purchases; it does not receive your full payment-card or bank details.

Storage, security and retention

Community and account data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the European multi-region eur3. We keep it while your anonymous account exists or while needed to operate, secure and moderate the service. Aggregated analytics and advertising records follow the retention settings of the relevant Google services. Technical safeguards and access rules restrict private account data to its owner, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You can change advertising privacy choices, turn off notifications and stop sharing location in the app. Profile → Delete my account removes your anonymous Firebase account, prayers, activity, settings and stored push values. Apple subscription records and aggregated or legally required records may remain with their respective providers.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children for contact information. If you believe a child has submitted personal information in a prayer, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
